Passing down...


Interviewer: For you, is keeping an object more about a sense of security, because it allows you to hold on to a memory?

Mary: I keep things because I like them and think they're valuable. Looking through them makes me feel happy.


Interviewer: What do you think will eventually happen to these things in the future?

Mary: I think I'll keep them forever.


Interviewer: If you have children or younger relatives someday, and they come across the things you've kept, how would you want them to understand you through those objects? Would you tell them the stories behind them?

Mary: Yes. I would share the stories so they could understand which things and memories are most important to me and what makes me happy. In this way, they would get to know me and understand my past more deeply.


Interviewer: Would you let them keep those things for you?

Mary: Yes.

Mary: I have a dinosaur doll that I used to sleep with. It's always been with me, so I hope it can continue to be with my child.


Interviewer: What does the doll look like?

Mary: It's a fluffy green dinosaur.


Interviewer: Thank you for sharing your thoughts about why we keep things. Have a good one!

Mary: Thank you!
