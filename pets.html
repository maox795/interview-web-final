Things They Leave Behind


Interviewer: Have you ever had a pet? Do you keep anything that belonged to them?

Mary: I have pets, and I keep their toys and clothes.


Interviewer: Even while your pet is still with you, do you find yourself wanting to keep something to remember them by when they're gone?

Mary: I have two pets. One is still with me, and I will keep the things the other one left behind because pets have very short lives. They always leave us first, so I try to keep something to remember them by.


Interviewer: Do you ever imagine the day when your other pet passes away, even though it's still healthy?

Mary: I don't imagine it in my mind, but if my pet passes away, I think I can accept it.
