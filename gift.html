About gifts


Interviewer: What other things do you keep from your parents, relatives or friends?

Mary: I have many toys from my childhood that my parents bought for me.


Interviewer: What specific toys?

Mary: Stickers, dolls, and puzzles.


Interviewer: I think every child has similar toys. What about things from your friends?

Mary: I keep almost all the gifts from my friends, but the things I treasure most are the handwritten letters they wrote to me.


Interviewer: That's sweet.
