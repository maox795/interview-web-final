The Memories and Meaning of Travel


Interviewer: What about things from your travels?

Mary: I don't really like buying souvenirs when I travel. If I'm not interested in something, I won't buy it. I don't deliberately buy things just to preserve a memory.


Interviewer: I'm the kind of person who likes to preserve memories intentionally. I would buy fridge magnets wherever I go because I think they represent the place. If you don't buy souvenirs, do you take a lot of pictures when you travel, or do you prefer to be present and enjoy the scenery?

Mary: I take pictures of my favorite places, beautiful landscapes, and small animals I meet.


Interviewer: That's cute.
