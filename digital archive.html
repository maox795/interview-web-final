An Archive of Us


Interviewer: Is it similar to your phone's photo album? Is there a specific photo you would never delete, even if it's not a great shot?

Mary: I hardly ever delete my photos, even unattractive selfies. I want to keep every photo I take in life so I can look back on them many years later.


Interviewer: So you keep almost everything. Is there a specific moment or memory you want to share?

Mary: I like keeping photos from hanging out with my friends, celebrating birthdays together, and going to concerts.


Interviewer: Who did you go to the concert with?

Mary: I went with my parents, but I watched the concert alone. It was a concert by my favorite Korean hip-hop group, NCT DREAM.


Interviewer: Who is your favorite member?

Mary: My bias in NCT DREAM is Jaemin.


Interviewer: How many photos do you have in your album?

Mary: About 70,000.


Interviewer: That's crazy. You're the person I've met with the most photos in their album. What about your chat history? Do you delete it, or do you keep everything?

Mary: I keep my chat history with everyone because I want to remember my interactions with each person in my life.


Interviewer: Is there any particular conversation you deliberately want to keep because it's especially meaningful?

Mary: I keep conversations with my dad, my mom, and my best friends, but there isn't one specific chat that matters the most. I want to keep every conversation because I want to look at how people grow and change over time.


Interviewer: Do you think keeping a photo or a conversation is the same as keeping a physical object?

Mary: I think they're the same. When you look at a photo or an object, they both spark memories and bring back certain feelings. They have the same effect.
